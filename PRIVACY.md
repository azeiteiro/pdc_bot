# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 2026

This Telegram bot is a private tool built for personal use within a closed group of festival attendees. It is not a public service.

## Data Collected

The bot collects and stores the following information from users who interact with it:

- **Telegram user ID and username** — used to identify users within the bot
- **Name, arrival/departure dates, and travel details** — collected during the onboarding flow and stored to coordinate festival logistics
- **Photos and videos** shared in the group chat — uploaded to a private Google Photos album
- **Expenses** added via bot commands — stored in a private Google Sheets spreadsheet

## How Data Is Used

All data is used exclusively to coordinate a private music festival among a known group of people. It is not shared with third parties, sold, or used for any commercial purpose.

## Data Storage

- A local SQLite database stores user session and onboarding status
- Google Sheets stores onboarding responses and expense records
- Google Photos stores media shared in the group

All storage is controlled by the bot operator and accessible only to them.

## Third-Party Services

The bot integrates with the following services:

- [Telegram Bot API](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api)
- [Google Photos API](https://developers.google.com/photos)
- [Google Sheets API](https://developers.google.com/sheets)
- [AccuWeather API](https://developer.accuweather.com)

Each service is governed by its own privacy policy and terms of service.

## Contact

This bot is operated privately. For any questions, contact the bot operator directly via Telegram.
